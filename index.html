How it works

  1. Open the free calculator and select the job type.
  2. Enter your actual costs and rates. All default values are illustrative.
  3. Check the quote, edit the scope yourself and copy the breakdown.

Tax note: Only use the VAT option when appropriate to the actual VAT registration and supply. The calculator is not tax or accounting advice.
