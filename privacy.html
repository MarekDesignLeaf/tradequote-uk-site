Privacy information

Last updated 13 October 2026.

TradeQuote UK is an early-stage independent project operated by Marek Sima. The free calculator runs entirely inside your browser. It does not submit job entries, customer details or prices to a server; entered values are discarded when you close or reload the page. No analytics, advertising pixels or cookies are intentionally installed in this site.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical request information, including IP addresses and server logs, to deliver the pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions or data rights requests, email marek@designleaf.co.uk. If you email us, your message and address are used to answer your enquiry. We do not automatically add you to a mailing list.

This is a free demo, not a payment site. No checkout is active.
